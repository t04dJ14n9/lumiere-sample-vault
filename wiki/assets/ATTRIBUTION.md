# Attribution

Everything in this repository is CC0, except the seven papers below. They belong to their authors and are redistributed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), each at the arXiv version named. Four carry annotations added by this repository with `tools/annotate_papers.py`; the notes in those annotations are this repository's own (CC0). No other change was made to any paper.
