# LLM Study

## Scope

Understanding how language models are trained and served well enough to train a small one end to end, and keeping up with what changes.

## Standards

- Every note links its sources; numbers come with a link to where they were read.
- A note is `mature` only once I could explain it without looking.

- [ ] Weekly: read the [nanochat](/wiki/resources/notes/nanochat.md) README's leaderboard for new rows
- [ ] Weekly: tick what I can now explain in the [learning path](/wiki/resources/notes/Learning-Path-LLMs.md)
